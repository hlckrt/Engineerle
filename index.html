ENGINEERLE Year 12 syllabus ↗

What am I?

Guess the engineering concept from five clues. The sooner you solve it, the better your score.

How to play

  1. Read the first clue and search for a concept.
  2. Guess, or reveal another clue if you need help.
  3. Use fewer clues to earn a higher score.

Questions are based on the Year 12 syllabus.
